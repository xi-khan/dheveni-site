How it works

Three things happen before anyone sees you

In that order, every time, for everybody. There is no way to skip one or buy past one.

Your ID is checked

Photograph your card. We read your name, birth date and sex, then delete the image within seconds. A one-way code keeps the same card from opening two accounts.

You talk, we listen

Your profile comes out of a conversation about faith, family, children and money. Not a form with tick boxes. You read it and approve it before anyone else does.

Someone vouches

A person who actually knows you says so. Until they do, nobody can find you. After they do, you are visible two steps out and no further.

Guardians

Your family can be in the room

Add a guardian to any conversation and they can read it and speak in it, in their own colour, so nobody is ever unsure who is talking. Either side can add one, and either side can remove theirs.
